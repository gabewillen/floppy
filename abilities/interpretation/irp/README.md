# `interpretation` implementation report plan

The implementation report plan of [`interpretation.scx`](../interpretation.scx): the W3C SCXML IRP's
manifest format and `Outcome` oracle over the document's numbered
statements below, one assertion each in [`manifest.xml`](manifest.xml),
tested by the harness documents in [`sources/`](sources). Each harness
invokes the document as its child, drives it, and ends in `pass` (logging
`Outcome` `pass`) or `fail`. `tests/tests/document_irp.rs` runs every case
through `scx run` with the real invoked services (a case's
`backend.json` scripts the fake HTTP backend they reach), and
`tests/irp/harness.py` writes the cases from [`cases.py`](cases.py).

## Statements

- <a id="interpretation-1"></a>**interpretation-1**. On start it sends `bot.ability.ready` to its parent (decision D242), with no data: it offers nothing.
- <a id="interpretation-2"></a>**interpretation-2**. The configuration's refusals (decision D177): a negative `product_threshold_db` and a `recognize_timeout_ms` that is not positive each end the interpretation in its final state `degraded` without offering; voice identification (`voice_classifier`) with speech decoding (`speech_decoder`) is no refusal.
- <a id="interpretation-3"></a>**interpretation-3**. With the default configuration (voice detection, sound classification, speech decoding), a voiced scored sound opens the open-speech window, each further voiced sound joins it, and the first sound without voice closes it; the utterance is joined into a WAV file, decoded, and answered as `bot.ability.listening.speech.output` `{content: <words>, content_type: "text/plain", voice_detection, sample_rate_hz, channels, media_type: "audio/pcm", source_ids: [], parent}`, its parent the loudest frame's (the first with a level when none is louder).
- <a id="interpretation-4"></a>**interpretation-4**. A sound without voice is classified: a labelled one (a non-blank kind) is handed to the parent as `bot.ability.cognition.input` `{stimulus}`, the stimulus `{event: "environment.sound", data: <the sound as voice detection normalized it, its PCM written beside it as floppy-sound-<n>.pcm>, source: "listening"}`; an unlabelled one is dropped while speech decoding is configured.
- <a id="interpretation-5"></a>**interpretation-5**. A product is handed off only when audible: its `perceived_level_db` null or at least `product_threshold_db` (3 dB); a quieter one is dropped.
- <a id="interpretation-6"></a>**interpretation-6**. With neither speech decoding nor sound classification, a sound without voice is a silence observation: a speech product of the sound's PCM (`content` its floppy-sound-<n>.pcm, `content_type` `audio/pcm`, no voice spans); a PCM sound with no sample rate is handed off as the `environment.sound` stimulus instead. While the window is open and neither decoding nor diarization owns the product, each frame (the closing one too) is streamed as such a speech product with its voice spans.
- <a id="interpretation-7"></a>**interpretation-7**. With diarization the closed utterance is diarized: one speech product per clipped segment (`content` floppy-diarized-<n>-<index>, its span, `confidence`, a `voice_detection` of its own length, and no source ids), or, with voice identification too, each identified (`source_ids` `[voice:<id>]`, its voice's ref); with speech decoding too, the utterance is decoded instead.
- <a id="interpretation-8"></a>**interpretation-8**. Voice identification without diarization identifies the opening voice span directly: its one embedding is identified and the voice's ref is the window's source identity, carried by every streamed frame; a sound that is not PCM with a sample rate cannot be identified and fails the `voice_identification` stage. Identification waits for its voices at most `stage_timeout_ms` + `recognize_timeout_ms` (2 s past the stage bound by default, decision D177); past that, or with no opening embedding, it completes with nobody identified: the window opens with no source identity, or each diarized segment is handed off with nobody.
- <a id="interpretation-9"></a>**interpretation-9**. A stage failure (its child's failure, an error, or its timer) answers `bot.ability.listening.failed` `{stage, message}` and returns to `Idle`, where the next sound is interpreted; decoded speech of a sound with no sample rate fails the `speech_decoding` stage.
- <a id="interpretation-10"></a>**interpretation-10**. Scored sounds that arrive while one is being interpreted are held (`<event:defer/>`) and taken in order.
- <a id="interpretation-11"></a>**interpretation-11**. Each stage but identification (statement 8) is bounded by `stage_timeout_ms` (30 s by default): its timer fails it with `{stage, message: "Listening <stage> timed out."}`.
- <a id="interpretation-12"></a>**interpretation-12**. The bot's own voice arriving as predicted (a scored sound that is `producing` with a `perceived_level_db` below `product_threshold_db`) is not interpreted (#30): it opens no utterance and does not join the open one, so the person's words around it are one utterance of their own. A sound heard while producing with no level, or at least at the threshold, is interpreted as usual.
- <a id="interpretation-13"></a>**interpretation-13**. With speech decoding, diarization and voice identification (the document's defaults), the decoded utterance's diarized segments of at least `min_voice_seconds` (1 s), else the longest, are embedded while its words are decoded (alongside diarization, decoding starts when the utterance is joined) and identified once the words are in: each voice that is not the bot's own is one of the decoded product's `source_ids` by its ref `voice:<id>`, and the product carries no embedding.
- <a id="interpretation-15"></a>**interpretation-15**. Identification is mosfet.py's (decision D253): the interpretation's voice registry matches each embedding to an id, or mints one (32 hex digits), so an embedding never leaves the interpretation: a voice heard again, at least `match_policy.threshold` (0.50) alike and clear of the next by its margin (0.05), is named by the same ref, and a voice unlike every id by a new one.
- <a id="interpretation-14"></a>**interpretation-14**. The bot's own voice is heard from speaking's copy of a completed utterance, `bot.ability.speaking.efference` with `audio` (its PCM, `media_type` `audio/pcm` with a sample rate), taken while voice identification is configured and the own voice is unknown: its voice is detected, and when at least `min_voice_seconds` (1 s) lies from its first voice to its last, that span is identified and enrolled as an own id; a copy with less voice is logged `interpretation.unidentified`, a copy without audio is nothing, and without voice identification the copy is ignored. A decoded voice recognized as the own id is not a source id.
- <a id="interpretation-15"></a>**interpretation-15**. With an affect classifier (`affect_classifier`, a document default), decoded speech carries the tone of its longest voice (mosfet.py's `SpeechData.affect`), heard through `abilities/voice_affect/voice_affect.scx` alongside decoding: the utterance, or with diarization its diarized segments; the product's `affect` is `{label, scores, valence, confidence}`. A tone not heard (a failure, a voice under 0.3 s, no answer within 3 s) leaves the product without `affect`, and the words still flow.
