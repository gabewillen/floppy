# `communication` implementation report plan

The implementation report plan of [`communication.scx`](../communication.scx): the W3C SCXML IRP's
manifest format and `Outcome` oracle over the document's numbered
statements below, one assertion each in [`manifest.xml`](manifest.xml),
tested by the harness documents in [`sources/`](sources). Each harness
invokes the document as its child, drives it, and ends in `pass` (logging
`Outcome` `pass`) or `fail`. `tests/tests/document_irp.rs` runs every case
through `scx run` with the real invoked services (a case's
`backend.json` scripts the fake HTTP backend they reach), and
`tests/irp/harness.py` writes the cases from [`cases.py`](cases.py).

## Statements

- <a id="communication-1"></a>**communication-1**. It is ready once both its children, the conversation and speaking, are ready (each sends `bot.ability.ready`): it sends `bot.ability.ready` to its parent, once and with no data (decision D242). What it offers in active, read from its live session (`<decision:offers>`), is the one model-callable `bot.ability.communication.respond`, of no effort, with its schema, no target.
- <a id="communication-2"></a>**communication-2**. `bot.ability.communication.input` in active, or in responding while a response is said (hearing goes on, decision D175), goes to the conversation; a text product comes back to the body as `bot.ability.cognition.input` `{stimulus}` unchanged, its stimulus carrying the conversation's current exchange, which the input opens as its one, inbound, message, stamped with the turn's participants (its source and target ids).
- <a id="communication-3"></a>**communication-3**. A product that is not text reaches the body as the conversation's `bot.ability.conversation.output` itself (its latest message inbound); an exchange whose latest message is outbound (the record of a response) is not forwarded.
- <a id="communication-4"></a>**communication-4**. A conversation that cannot take an input fails it: its `bot.ability.conversation.failed` `{stage, message}` goes on to the body unchanged.
- <a id="communication-5"></a>**communication-5**. `bot.ability.communication.respond` `{parts}` (1 to 64 parts `{expression?, words?}`, decision D178) that says something, in active, is said through speaking: in order, the body gets speaking's efference copies `bot.ability.speaking.efference` `{mouth: "speaking", duration, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels}` (the first before the sound, then one per piece played), the communication puts speaking's `environment.sound` `{audio, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels, source: "speaking"}` into the environment whose id its `environment` names (`#_scxml_` + that id), and the body gets `bot.ability.speaking.output` `{text, spoken, unspoken, outcome, voice_id, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels}`, its text the parts' words joined exactly as written; the communication is active again and offers respond again, and the outbound record joins the conversation's exchange only.
- <a id="communication-6"></a>**communication-6**. A reply that says nothing (its words, joined, blank; a part whose words are blank; a part with neither words, a pause nor a sound; an expression that directs no cue) answers `bot.ability.communication.failed` `{message: "A reply must say something: every part needs words, a sound or a pause, and the words must not be blank."}` without speaking; the communication stays active and says the next response. A respond its schema refuses (no parts, empty words) never arrives: the send raises `error.execution` in the sender.
- <a id="communication-7"></a>**communication-7**. A speaking failure answers `bot.ability.communication.failed` `{message}` (speaking's failure message) and the communication is active again.
- <a id="communication-8"></a>**communication-8**. A response is bounded by `response_timeout_ms` (36000 by default, a `<param>`), which bounds speaking's silence on a response: each `bot.ability.speaking.progress` re-arms it (D179). When it passes before speaking answers, the body gets `bot.ability.communication.failed` `{message: "Speaking response timed out."}` and the communication is active again; speaking's late answer is not forwarded. `encoding_timeout_ms` (30000 by default, a `<param>`; decision D98) is passed to speaking as its encoding bound.
- <a id="communication-9"></a>**communication-9**. Responses that arrive while a response is in hand, and input that arrives before it is active, are deferred and taken, in order, once it is active.
- <a id="communication-10"></a>**communication-10**. `bot.ability.communication.activate` `{}` is taken only in inactive: in active it does nothing.
- <a id="communication-11"></a>**communication-11**. A response's id is its respond's sendid (none when the sender names none). A finished response's id is kept among the last `closed_responses_bound` (16) finished ids, and a respond with a finished id, or another copy of the response in hand or of a held one, is dropped (logged `communication.late`): nothing is said or answered for it, and a re-sent held id takes no second slot toward `held_responses_bound` (the first copy is kept, decision D175).
- <a id="communication-12"></a>**communication-12**. At most `held_responses_bound` (16, a `<param>`) responses are held while one is in hand; past that a respond is refused with `bot.ability.communication.failed` `{message: "Communication is holding too many responses; this one is refused."}` (decision D175), and the held ones are still said, in order.
- <a id="communication-13"></a>**communication-13**. While a response is said (responding) it offers, read from its live session, respond (held) and the model-callable `bot.ability.communication.stop` `{}` (decision D179). A stop while a response is said sends speaking `bot.ability.speaking.stop` `{}`, which stops it: the body gets the efference copy `{duration: 0}` and `bot.ability.speaking.output` with `outcome` `stopped`, and the communication is active again.
- <a id="communication-14"></a>**communication-14**. Speaking's `bot.ability.speaking.progress` re-arms the response's hop timer (decision D179), and the hop timeout also sends speaking `bot.ability.speaking.stop` `{}`.
