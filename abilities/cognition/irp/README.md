# `cognition` implementation report plan

The implementation report plan of [`cognition.scx`](../cognition.scx): the W3C SCXML IRP's
manifest format and `Outcome` oracle over the document's numbered
statements below, one assertion each in [`manifest.xml`](manifest.xml),
tested by the harness documents in [`sources/`](sources). Each harness
invokes the document as its child, drives it, and ends in `pass` (logging
`Outcome` `pass`) or `fail`. `tests/tests/document_irp.rs` runs every case
through `scx run` with the real invoked services (a case's
`backend.json` scripts the fake HTTP backend they reach), and
`tests/irp/harness.py` writes the cases from [`cases.py`](cases.py).

## Statements

- <a id="cognition-1"></a>**cognition-1**. On start it sends `bot.ability.ready` to its parent, once (never on a later return to `idle`) and with no data (decision D242). What it offers in `idle`, read from its live session (`<decision:offers>`), is its one choice `bot.ability.cognition.ignore`, of no effort, with its schema as written (its parent makes it model-facing).
- <a id="cognition-2"></a>**cognition-2**. A `bot.ability.cognition.input` `{turn, stimulus, focus?, focus_candidates?, choices?}` runs one turn of the cascade: autonomy (without a database it leaves the turn unhandled), then the decision layer, whose confident decision handles the turn: `bot.ability.cognition.output` `{turn, output}`, the output the decision layer's selections, which it has sent to their targets.
- <a id="cognition-3"></a>**cognition-3**. When the decision layer leaves the turn unhandled (its decision abstains), reasoning (the generate service, schema `dispatch`) handles it, and the output is reasoning's selections; with a database (the `<param>` `database`) autonomy and reasoning use the memory and the turn is answered the same way.
- <a id="cognition-4"></a>**cognition-4**. A decision layer that escalates for low confidence sends its environment actions (its selections) before the turn goes on to reasoning; the output is those actions (the turn's acted) followed by reasoning's selections, which reasoning sends after them, a repeat of an environment action included.
- <a id="cognition-5"></a>**cognition-5**. A decision layer that fails (`the decision failed (status)`, or no decision within `decision_timeout_ms`) does not fail the turn: the cognition logs `cognition.cascaded` and reasoning answers it (decision D140). A reasoning stage that fails answers the turn with `bot.ability.failed` `{message, turn, input: {focus, focus_candidates}}`, the message the stage's reason (`the reasoning failed (status)`, or `Selection rejected: <message>` once a refused selection is not repaired, the dispatch tool's rejection message, decisions D126 and D142), then hands the failure back to its parent as a `bot.ability.cognition.input` whose stimulus is `bot.ability.cognition.step.failed` `{message: "Cognition's reasoning step failed, so the turn failed: <reason>", stage: "reasoning", turn}`.
- <a id="cognition-6"></a>**cognition-6**. A step that makes no progress for `child_operation_timeout_ms` (from its start for autonomy and the decision layer; for reasoning from its streamed reply's first `generate.progress`, each later one starting it over), or runs `child_operation_ceiling_ms` in all (the `<param>` `bounds` overriding either, decision D101, and its `reflex_timeout_ms`, `intuition_timeout_ms` or `reasoning_timeout_ms` bounding one tier), fails only its turn: `bot.ability.failed` `{message: "Cognition's <stage> step took longer than <seconds> seconds, so the turn failed.", turn, input}`, then the failure handed back to its parent as a `bot.ability.cognition.input` whose stimulus is `bot.ability.cognition.step.timed_out` `{message, stage, timeout_seconds, turn}`; the cognition is idle again. A turn whose stimulus is a handed-back failure hands nothing back when it fails too.
- <a id="cognition-7"></a>**cognition-7**. `bot.ability.cognition.cancel` `{turn}` for the turn in hand cancels the stage and is acknowledged with `bot.ability.cognition.cancelled` `{turn}`, and the cognition is idle again; in `idle` a cancel is acknowledged at once; a cancel for another turn is not taken and the turn goes on.
- <a id="cognition-8"></a>**cognition-8**. `bot.ability.cognition.ignore` (its offer) is taken in `idle` and while processing, and only logged: it sends nothing and does not end a turn.
- <a id="cognition-11"></a>**cognition-11**. Until it is told the bot's name (`bot.ability.identity.named`), every return to `idle` sends `bot.ability.identity.ask` `{}` to its parent, the first after the one `bot.ability.ready` it sends on start; once told, it asks no more.
- <a id="cognition-10"></a>**cognition-10**. With an `intuition_model` (the `<param>`; none by default composes no intuition tier, decision D144), a turn the decision layer leaves unhandled goes to the intuition tier first, offered the deliberative handoff `bot.ability.reasoning.input` as the cognition's own: its selections handle the turn, or, when they hold the handoff, the others are sent first and the turn goes on to reasoning, whose output follows them. An intuition that fails hands the same turn on to reasoning (`cognition.cascaded`).
- <a id="cognition-9"></a>**cognition-9**. The events it takes are checked against `abilities/schemas/` where they are created: a `bot.ability.cognition.input` without a stimulus is refused at its sender (`error.execution` there) and starts no turn.
- <a id="cognition-12"></a>**cognition-12**. With no input for `rest_after_ms` (the `<param>`, else the `bounds`' `rest_after_ms`; 5 minutes by default) `idle` rests (decisions D195 and D205): it logs `cognition.rest` `resting` and invokes the consolidation with its database (with none there is nothing to consolidate, logged `cognition.consolidated`), and stays at rest until input, which leaves rest at once (`cognition.rest` `awake`, cancelling a consolidation in progress) and is taken as a normal turn.
- <a id="cognition-14"></a>**cognition-14**. Each tier is offered only the turn's choices within its effort ceiling (decisions D196 and D206): a choice's `effort` (0 without one) at most the decision layer's `decision_effort_ceiling` (0) or intuition's `intuition_effort_ceiling` (1), each the `bounds`' member of that name when it is a number, and every choice for reasoning, whose ceiling is the whole frame. What is over a tier's ceiling is not among its choices, so the tier cannot select it and only hands it on.
