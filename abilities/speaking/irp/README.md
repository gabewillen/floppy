# `speaking` implementation report plan

The implementation report plan of [`speaking.scx`](../speaking.scx): the W3C SCXML IRP's
manifest format and `Outcome` oracle over the document's numbered
statements below, one assertion each in [`manifest.xml`](manifest.xml),
tested by the harness documents in [`sources/`](sources). Each harness
invokes the document as its child, drives it, and ends in `pass` (logging
`Outcome` `pass`) or `fail`. `tests/tests/document_irp.rs` runs every case
through `scx run` with the real invoked services (a case's
`backend.json` scripts the fake HTTP backend they reach), and
`tests/irp/harness.py` writes the cases from [`cases.py`](cases.py).

## Statements

- <a id="speaking-1"></a>**speaking-1**. On start, and only then, it sends `bot.ability.ready` to its parent (decision D242), with no data: it offers nothing, and the end of an utterance sends nothing more.
- <a id="speaking-2"></a>**speaking-2**. `bot.ability.speaking.input` `{parts}` (1 to 64 parts `{expression?, words?}`, decision D178) is encoded by the vocal child, which gets the parts, with the speaking's `provider`, `model`, `voice` and `media_type` (by default the OpenAI-compatible encoder, provider `null`, model `sidekick-tts-engine-breeze`, voice `warm`, `audio/pcm`), written to `output_prefix` + the utterance's number + `.` + the media subtype (`media/speaking-<n>.pcm`); the utterance's text of record is the parts' words joined exactly as written (nothing stripped).
- <a id="speaking-3"></a>**speaking-3**. `playing` sends the efference copy `bot.ability.speaking.efference` `{mouth: "speaking", duration, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels}` before the sound, `environment.sound` `{audio, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels, source: "speaking"}` (its source the copy's mouth, so the listening can tell its own voice apart), and `bot.ability.speaking.progress` `{spoken_seconds: 0}`, to its parent; with no measured duration no copy is sent, and a rate or channel count the encoder leaves null is the speaking's own (24000 Hz mono).
- <a id="speaking-4"></a>**speaking-4**. `recording` sends `bot.ability.conversation.append` `{message}` to its parent, the outbound message `<conversation:message_for_turn>` builds (content what was spoken, content_type `text/plain`, participants `[]` until the speaking has a voice of its own, provenance id `speaking-<n>` and event `bot.ability.conversation.append`), unless nothing was spoken, then answers `bot.ability.speaking.output` `{text, spoken, unspoken, outcome, voice_id: null, media_type, sample_rate_hz, channels}` (decision D179) and returns to `idle`.
- <a id="speaking-5"></a>**speaking-5**. An utterance that says nothing (its words, joined, blank; a part whose words are blank; a part with neither words, a pause nor a sound; an expression that directs no cue) is refused before any encoding: `bot.ability.failed` `{message: "A reply must say something: every part needs words, a sound or a pause, and the words must not be blank."}`, and back to `idle`; one its schema refuses (no parts, empty words) never arrives: the send raises `error.execution` in the sender.
- <a id="speaking-6"></a>**speaking-6**. A vocal failure (its `bot.ability.failed`) answers the parent with `bot.ability.failed` `{message}` carrying the vocal's message, and back to `idle`, where the next input is spoken.
- <a id="speaking-7"></a>**speaking-7**. Encoding is bounded by `encoding_timeout_ms` (30 s by default, decision D98): its timer answers `bot.ability.failed` `{message: "Speech encoding timed out."}` and returns to `idle`.
- <a id="speaking-8"></a>**speaking-8**. Input for another utterance that arrives outside `idle` is held (`<event:defer/>`), up to `held_utterances_bound` (16, a `<param>`), and taken, in order, on the return to `idle`; past that bound it is refused with `bot.ability.failed` `{message: "Speaking is holding too many utterances; this one is refused."}` (decision D175).
- <a id="speaking-9"></a>**speaking-9**. An utterance's id is its input's sendid (none when the sender names none). A finished utterance's id is kept among the last `finished_utterances_bound` (16) finished ids, and an input with a finished id, or another copy of the utterance in hand or of a held one, is dropped (logged `speaking.late`): nothing is said or answered for it, and a re-sent held id takes no second slot toward `held_utterances_bound` (the first copy is kept, decision D175).
- <a id="speaking-10"></a>**speaking-10**. Playout lasts as long as the measured audio: the mouth's report of each piece (at most half of `lookahead_ms`, 1000; `devices.audio.played` `{mark}`, which the speaking times with `<send delay>` until the mouth is ported) is followed by the efference copy of the playout still to come and `bot.ability.speaking.progress` `{spoken_seconds}` (the seconds reported played); after the last piece, the progress and then the copy `{duration: 0}`, and the utterance is recorded, `outcome` `completed`, everything spoken. Audio with no measured duration counts as played at once.
- <a id="speaking-11"></a>**speaking-11**. `bot.ability.speaking.stop` `{}` stops the utterance in hand: in encoding nothing is said; in playing what was said is the words the reported pieces cover, in proportion to the share of the audio played (of the audio received, while the encoding still streams), rounded down to whole words, and the encoding still under way is cancelled. Either way the efference copy `{duration: 0}` leaves at once, the utterance is recorded with `outcome` `stopped` (appended to the conversation only when something was spoken), and the speaking is idle again. A stop with nothing in hand does nothing (logged `speaking.stale`).
- <a id="speaking-12"></a>**speaking-12**. The encoding streams (decision D203): playout starts on vocal's first `bot.ability.vocal.speech.encoding.chunk`, before its output, with the copy of the audio received so far; each chunk is its own `environment.sound` (its audio the chunk's file) when its turn comes, in order, and is played piece by piece; chunks that arrive meanwhile wait their turn, and once the output has arrived and its last chunk played the copy is `{duration: 0}` and the utterance is recorded.
- <a id="speaking-13"></a>**speaking-13**. Until the speaking has a voice of its own (`voice_id` null), recording a completed utterance of at least 1.5 s of PCM also sends its parent `bot.ability.listening.enroll` `{voice_id: null, segment: {audio, media_type: "audio/pcm", sample_rate_hz, channels, start_seconds: 0, end_seconds: <its duration>}}`, the copy of what it said; a shorter one sends none. `bot.ability.listening.enrolled` `{voice_id}`, taken while `voice_id` is null, is the speaking's own voice: from then on it sends no copy, its outbound messages carry the participant `{ref: <voice_id>, role: "source", own: true}`, and its output's `voice_id` is that id.
